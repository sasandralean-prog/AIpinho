# AIpinho Truth Audit

Use this skill when reviewing a diff, report, wave, or proposed verdict for
truth/governance defects.

## Inspect

- false success;
- candidate/Truth collapse;
- observed/derived collapse;
- unknown/false collapse;
- missing observation becoming invented data;
- evidence and provenance preservation;
- claim-level evidence binding;
- terminality and exactly one terminal event;
- stage-specific reasons versus generic timeout;
- validation, completion, and SpeakerTruth semantics;
- artifact semantics;
- runtime-agent versus engineering-agent authority confusion;
- local/cloud proof overclaiming.

## Output

Lead with blockers and evidence. State whether the issue is P0, P1, P2, or P3,
and whether the proof is `not_proven`, `probable_with_evidence`, or `proven`.
